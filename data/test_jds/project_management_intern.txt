Project Management Intern (6-month placement)

About the role
Our IT delivery office runs internal software projects for a large Irish insurance company. We are looking for an organised student to support our project managers.

What you will do
- Help plan project timelines and track progress in Jira
- Organise meetings, take minutes and follow up on actions
- Keep the risk and issue log up to date
- Prepare weekly status updates for stakeholders
- Support user acceptance testing with business teams

Requirements
- Excellent written and verbal communication
- Strong organisation and time management
- Ability to work with people across technical and business teams
- Problem solving and the confidence to raise risks early
- Leadership potential and teamwork

Nice to have
- Experience with Jira, Trello or similar tools
- Knowledge of Agile or Scrum
