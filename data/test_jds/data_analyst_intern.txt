Data Analyst Intern (6-month placement)

About the role
Our analytics team supports an Irish retail group with reports on sales, stock and customer behaviour. We are looking for a student on placement to help turn data into decisions.

What you will do
- Write SQL queries to pull and clean data from our data warehouse
- Build and update dashboards in Power BI
- Analyse trends in Excel and present findings to store and marketing managers
- Check data quality and document how each report is calculated

Requirements
- Strong SQL skills, including joins and aggregations
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, lookups, charts)
- Understanding of basic statistics (averages, distributions, correlation)
- Ability to explain findings clearly to non-technical colleagues
- Attention to detail

Nice to have
- Power BI or Tableau
- Python for data analysis (pandas)
